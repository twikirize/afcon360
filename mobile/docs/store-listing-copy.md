# AFCON360 draft store listing copy

These are editorial drafts for the separate User and Driver products. Review product claims, country coverage, support contacts, character limits, screenshots and privacy disclosures before pasting into Play Console or App Store Connect.

## AFCON360 User

**Suggested app name:** AFCON360 User

**Short description (draft):** Rides, stays and East African experiences in one travel app.

**Full description (draft):**

Plan your journeys across East Africa with AFCON360.

AFCON360 User brings your travel essentials together in one place. Search for a pickup and destination, compare transport fare options, choose an available payment method and follow your ride request as AFCON360 finds and assigns a driver. Once a driver and vehicle are confirmed, view the assignment details in the app.

You can also explore accommodation, tourism and events through the connected AFCON360 marketplace, review your recent ride activity and see the balance returned by AFCON360 Wallet.

AFCON360 services and provider availability vary by location. Fare quotes, payment eligibility and payment status are confirmed by AFCON360 services. Indicative home-currency displays are not a promise of a converted settlement amount.

Sign in with your existing AFCON360 account. Account settings include sign-out and an account-deletion request.

**Suggested category:** Travel. Confirm final classification in each store console.

## AFCON360 Driver

**Suggested app name:** AFCON360 Driver

**Short description (draft):** Ride offers, trip tools and earnings for approved AFCON360 drivers.

**Full description (draft):**

A dedicated work app for approved AFCON360 drivers.

Use AFCON360 Driver to review your operational readiness, change availability when your account is eligible, receive and respond to ride offers, and update the status of an assigned trip. Your driver profile, compliance, vehicle eligibility, offer assignment and trip transitions remain controlled by AFCON360.

The app displays captured trip earnings grouped by currency. Cash collection is recorded only after the trip is complete and the driver confirms that cash was actually received.

When you choose to share your current position, AFCON360 Driver asks for location permission while you are using the app and sends the location for transport operations. This version does not enable continuous background location tracking.

Sign in with your existing approved AFCON360 Driver account. Driver access and services depend on AFCON360 verification and operating eligibility.

**Suggested category:** Business or Maps & Navigation. Confirm classification with the store reviewer and actual primary functionality.
