When contacting us

Please include a short description of the issue and, where relevant, your booking reference or transaction reference. Do not email passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details or wallet PINs.

For a privacy request, tell us what you would like to access, correct or have reviewed. We may need to verify your identity before responding to protect your information.

Urgent safety matters

AFCON360 is not an emergency-response service. If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical, police or fire assistance, contact the relevant local emergency service first. You can then notify AFCON360 about a related platform or provider issue.
