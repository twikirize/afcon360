Cookie Policy

This page explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit AFCON360 online.

WORKING DRAFT — VERIFY AGAINST THE LIVE WEBSITE The actual cookie names, providers, durations, analytics tools and consent controls must be checked in the deployed website before this policy is approved. Remove any category that is not used.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies may store or read information on a device. They can help a website remember a session, protect an account or understand how a service is used.

2. Types of technology that may be used

  • Essential cookies: support core functions such as sign-in sessions, security, request protection and navigation. These may be necessary for the website to work.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices such as language or display settings, where these features are enabled.
  • Analytics and performance tools: may help understand traffic, errors and feature usage. They should be enabled and configured in accordance with applicable law and any required consent.
  • Third-party content: maps, media or embedded services may use their own technologies when loaded. Review the provider's notice where relevant.

3. Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in or other website features from working properly. Where consent is required for optional technologies, the website should provide an appropriate choice and respect that choice.

4. Updates and questions

We may update this policy when the website's technologies change. For questions, visit Contact Us or read the Privacy Policy.
