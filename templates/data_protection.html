Data Protection

How AFCON360 intends to handle personal information responsibly across accounts, bookings, providers, payments and support.

WORKING DRAFT — OPERATIONAL AND LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED This page is a plain-language summary, not a certification or guarantee. Retain only statements that match AFCON360's actual policies, technical controls, contracts and operating practices.

Our approach

People should be able to understand why information is requested, how it supports a service and who may receive it. AFCON360 aims to collect information for clear purposes, limit access to people and systems that need it, keep it only as long as necessary and respond responsibly to questions or concerns.

Practical data-protection principles

Purpose and transparencyExplain what is collected and why, using clear notices at the point where information is requested.
Data minimisationAsk for information that is relevant to the feature or transaction, not information that is merely convenient to collect.
Access controlLimit staff, provider and system access according to their responsibilities and legitimate need.
Retention and disposalUse approved retention periods and securely dispose of information when it is no longer required.

Information that needs special care

Account credentials, identity-verification material, precise location, booking details and transaction records can be sensitive in context. Access and sharing should be restricted to the purpose for which the information is needed. Never send passwords, one-time passcodes, wallet PINs or full payment-card details to AFCON360 support by email.

Providers and technology partners

Some services require information to be shared with a provider or technology partner. For example, a transport provider may need pickup and trip details to fulfil a ride, while a payment provider may need transaction information to complete a payment. AFCON360 should use appropriate agreements, access limits and supplier reviews for these relationships.

Security measures

Depending on the system and risk, safeguards may include role-based access, authentication controls, secure communications, security logging, monitoring, backups, software maintenance and incident-response procedures. This summary does not claim that AFCON360 holds a particular security certification or that every safeguard applies to every provider.

Requests and concerns

For questions about information held by AFCON360, or to ask for access, correction or deletion where available under applicable law, contact compliance@afcon360.com. We may ask for reasonable identity verification before disclosing or changing personal information.

For suspected account compromise or a security vulnerability, use security@afcon360.com. Do not include passwords, live access tokens or other secrets in your report.
