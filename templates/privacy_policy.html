Privacy Policy

This draft explains, in plain language, the types of information AFCON360 may handle and the choices available to people who use the website, applications and related services.

Before publication as the final policy, confirm the responsible legal entity, its address, the actual service providers, processing locations, lawful bases, retention periods and country-specific rights and complaint channels.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

AFCON360 is operated by [INSERT FULL REGISTERED LEGAL ENTITY NAME], of [INSERT REGISTERED ADDRESS] (referred to as “AFCON360”, “we”, “us” or “our”). The correct entity and whether it acts as a data controller or processor for each activity must be confirmed before this policy is finalised.

This policy is intended to cover the AFCON360 website, user and driver applications, account services, transport and accommodation journeys, tours and experiences, event-related services, wallet features and customer support, to the extent those services are made available.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account and profile information: name, username, email address, telephone number, country, profile details, account preferences and verification status.
  • Identity and eligibility information: information or documents requested for account security, provider onboarding, verification or legal requirements, where applicable.
  • Bookings and service information: event interests, transport requests, pickup and destination details, accommodation requests, tour selections, booking references, provider communications and service history.
  • Location information: place searches, selected locations and, where a feature requires it and permission is given, device or driver location. The purpose and timing of location collection should be described in the relevant permission prompt.
  • Wallet and transaction information: wallet identifiers, balances, invoices, payment status, amounts, currencies, transaction references and related records. Payment providers may separately process payment credentials.
  • Communications: messages sent to support, feedback, complaints, survey responses and correspondence with providers through the platform.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, device and browser information, app version, session and log events, security signals and diagnostic information.
  • Preferences and usage: saved choices, notification settings, consent records and information about how platform features are used.

We should collect only information that is relevant to the feature or purpose involved. The precise fields collected by each service must be confirmed against the live product before this policy is approved.

3. How we use information

Depending on the service, we may use information to:

  • create and protect accounts, authenticate users and manage permissions;
  • display events, destinations, listings and service options;
  • request quotes, coordinate providers, confirm bookings and support trip operations;
  • display wallet information, process or record payments, reconcile transactions and handle refunds;
  • send service messages, booking updates, security alerts and support responses;
  • prevent fraud, investigate misuse, protect users and maintain platform integrity;
  • improve reliability, troubleshoot errors and understand how features are used;
  • meet applicable legal, accounting, regulatory and dispute-resolution obligations; and
  • send optional news or marketing where permitted and where required choices or consent have been obtained.

4. Our reasons for processing

Depending on the country, service and circumstances, processing may be based on taking steps requested by you or performing a contract; meeting a legal obligation; your consent where consent is required; or legitimate interests such as protecting accounts, preventing fraud, maintaining the service and responding to enquiries, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

5. When we share information

We may share limited information where needed with:

  • service providers and partners involved in a booking or request, such as a transport operator, accommodation host, tour provider or event organiser;
  • payment, mobile-money, banking and other transaction providers involved in a payment or refund;
  • technology suppliers that support hosting, security, messaging, analytics, mapping, identity checks or customer support;
  • professional advisers, auditors and insurers where reasonably necessary;
  • public authorities, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or courts where disclosure is required or lawfully requested; and
  • another person where you ask us to share information or authorise the relevant connection.

We do not intend to make personal information publicly available merely because you use the platform. The actual provider and subprocessor list, including the role of each party, must be confirmed and added where appropriate.

6. Cross-border processing

AFCON360 is designed for services that may connect people and providers across East African countries. Information may therefore be accessed or processed in a country other than the one where you are located. Before launch, AFCON360 must document the countries involved, the parties receiving information and the safeguards or transfer arrangements required by applicable law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, account and service administration, payment and accounting records, safety, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations. When it is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of in line with the approved retention schedule.

8. Security

We seek to protect information through appropriate technical and organisational measures, which may include access controls, staff responsibilities, secure communications, monitoring, backups and procedures for managing security incidents. No website, application or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The final wording should be checked against the controls actually deployed.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may be able to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate details, deletion, restriction of processing, a copy of certain information, or object to certain uses. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

You can manage certain profile, notification, location-permission and device-session choices through the platform or your device settings. To make a privacy request, contact compliance@afcon360.com or use the Contact Us page. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children

AFCON360 services are not designed to encourage children to create accounts independently. Where a service or event involves a child, the applicable age requirements, parent or guardian involvement and event-organiser rules must be followed. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is not permitted, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support sessions, security and core website functions. Other technologies, such as preference or analytics tools, should be used in accordance with applicable law and any required consent choices. See the Cookie Policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The final published policy should show its effective date and a clear record of material updates.
