ℹ️ How it works:
  • Enabled - Payment method is globally enabled in the system
  • Active - Payment method is currently operational (may be disabled temporarily)
  • Marketplace Collection Implemented - Wallet has implemented the collection/settlement lifecycle for this method. Only methods with this enabled will appear in Transport, Accommodation, and Events payment selectors.
  • Cash and Wallet are pre-configured with marketplace collection implemented.
  • Mobile money, card gateways, and other methods require Wallet to implement their collection lifecycle before enabling this flag.
