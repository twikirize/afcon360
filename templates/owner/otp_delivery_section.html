OTP Delivery Methods Configuration
Configure how OTP codes are delivered to users. Enable the channels you want active. Fallback chain: Enabled channels are tried in order (SMS → WhatsApp → Email).
Each channel requires valid credentials to work.

Setup Instructions
  1. For Email: Configure OCI Email Delivery in OCI Console
  2. For SMS: Get Account SID and Auth Token from Twilio Console
  3. Buy or verify a phone number in Phone Numbers
  4. For WhatsApp: Request access in WhatsApp Senders or use sandbox (whatsapp:+14155238886)
  5. Fallback chain: Enabled channels tried in order (WhatsApp → SMS → Email)
