Set up a service area in four steps

1. Name the area

Use a city or operating-zone name and its country code

2. Find the place

Search for the city to centre the map; then zoom to the coverage you serve

3. Draw the boundary

Use the polygon tool for an accurate edge or the rectangle tool for a simple operating zone

4. Select services and save

Choose Transport or other applicable services. Enable enforcement only after reviewing every active area

The boundary is the area Pamoja supports, not a promise that every road is covered by the routing network. Use a realistic operating boundary and avoid drawing a single rectangle around an entire country.
