Setup Instructions
  1. Create a Twilio account or log in
  2. Get Account SID and Auth Token from Twilio Console
  3. Buy or verify a phone number in Phone Numbers
  4. For WhatsApp: Request access in WhatsApp Senders or use sandbox (whatsapp:+14155238886)
  5. Configure fallback chain: SMS → WhatsApp → Email (configured automatically)
