About Escrow Accounts
  • Escrow accounts hold funds until service delivery is confirmed.
  • Each service type can have one escrow account.
  • Funds are released to service providers upon completion.
  • All escrow transactions are audited and logged.
  • Frozen accounts cannot process transactions.
