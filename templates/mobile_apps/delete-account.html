Account and privacy controls

Request AFCON360 account deletion

This page is the web route for customers and drivers who wish to request closure of an AFCON360 account. You can also start deletion inside the User or Driver app under Account.

What happens when you submit a request?

  • Your sign-in is disabled and active website/mobile device sessions are revoked.
  • Direct account and general-profile identifiers are anonymized, and active MFA, recovery, PIN and personal API credentials are invalidated.
  • Some KYC/AML, wallet-ledger, booking, safety, dispute and audit records may be retained only where required for applicable legal, financial or safety obligations.
  • AFCON360 does not promise that statutory or financial records are erased where retention is legally required. The approved privacy notice will state the applicable categories and retention periods.

Re-enter your current password and, when enabled, your authenticator/backup code. Type the confirmation phrase exactly. This verifies your intent; it does not transfer, refund or close any outstanding financial obligation.
