Account and privacy controls

Request AFCON360 account deletion

This page is the web route for customers, providers and drivers who wish to close an AFCON360 account. You can also start closure inside the User or Driver app under Account.

What happens when you submit a request?

  • Your account is disabled immediately and active website and mobile sessions are revoked.
  • Your profile and public content are hidden during a {{ grace_days }}-day grace period.
  • You can cancel before the grace period ends using the link sent to your account email. Cancelling restores sign-in and account access.
  • At closure, direct account and general-profile identifiers are anonymized, and active MFA, recovery, PIN and personal API credentials are invalidated.
  • Wallet balances and pending payouts must be resolved before closure. Closure does not refund, transfer or cancel any outstanding financial obligation.
  • KYC/AML, wallet-ledger, booking, safety, dispute and audit records may be retained only where an applicable legal, financial, safety, security or dispute-resolution obligation requires it, and only for the period supported by that obligation. This flow does not promise a blanket retention period.
  • AFCON360 does not promise that statutory, financial or safety records are erased where retention is legally required.
  • Creating another account does not cancel or remove outstanding obligations attached to your previous account.

Re-enter your current password and, when enabled, your authenticator or backup code. Type the confirmation phrase exactly. Then tick every acknowledgement below. This verifies your intent; it does not transfer, refund or close any outstanding financial obligation.
