About AFCON360

One connected place to help fans and travellers discover, plan and coordinate the experiences around major events in East Africa.

What we are building

AFCON360 is a digital fan-support and services marketplace designed to make event travel easier to organise. We bring useful information and service discovery closer together, so people can explore transport, accommodation, tours, local experiences and event-related services without having to begin every part of a trip from scratch.

Our work is particularly focused on the East African region, including Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania, and on helping visitors and local communities connect around major events such as the Africa Cup of Nations 2027.

More than a booking page

Travel is made up of many connected decisions. A fan may need to understand an event, find a place to stay, arrange a ride, discover something to do and manage payments. AFCON360 is being developed to bring those journeys into a more connected experience.

How we work

We aim to make service information easier to understand, give local providers a way to reach travellers, and make the steps between discovery and a confirmed service clearer. Availability, fulfilment and service quality may depend on the independent provider or event organiser involved.

We are committed to improving the experience through clear information, responsible handling of personal data, accessible support and practical tools for fans, travellers and service partners.

Our relationship with tournaments and organisers

AFCON360 is a platform for fan support and related services. Unless a formal relationship is expressly announced by the relevant rights holder or organiser, AFCON360 should not be understood as the tournament organiser, a football governing body, an immigration authority or an official ticket issuer. Always check official event, travel and entry requirements with the responsible authorities.
